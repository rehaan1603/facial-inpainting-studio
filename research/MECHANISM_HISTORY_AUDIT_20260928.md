## Permitted initial screen and remaining uncertainty

The already observed development identities **1306,2790,1043,787** are the appropriate initial debugging/screen cohort because all recent missing-region controls and reference/gallery roles exist for them. Use their frozen four-reference/three-gallery assignment and masks; no new clean-target inspection should guide inference logic. Other already observed development groups include386,2087,2289,1485; their failures cannot be removed to make a screen easier.

Only a candidate that passes a separately frozen anti-regression screen can justify a separately frozen independent development confirmation. The metadata-only pool audit currently reports at most11 unused local validation groups with eight photos before duplicate/quality checks. Four official FFHQ-Ref validation groups of that size are author-predicted groups, not verified client identities. Neither source automatically supplies a large, pretraining-disjoint confirmation cohort.

The completed dedicated baseline shows blurred/ghosted eyes, gaze and spacing errors, smeared noses/lips, wrong teeth/open-mouth state and altered expression across all four people. A structural mechanism must distinguish relatively stable identity geometry from unobserved expression: the true hidden gaze or transient mouth state may be unidentifiable from unrelated reference photographs. The known component gate behavior is insufficient to defend a contribution by itself. Prior-art review and a prospective method specification must precede any new implementation or large run.
