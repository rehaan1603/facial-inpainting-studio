# Structural reference transfer: prior-art audit and bounded next test

Primary sources checked 27–28 September 2026; decision recorded 28 September 2026. This is a literature and mechanism assessment, not an experimental result. No photographs, reserved-final images, generators or evaluation models were opened or run for this review. Historical results and source checkouts are unchanged.

## Decision

**A broad claim to reference-guided facial structure, multiple-reference semantics, visible-region geometric fitting or landmark warping is not novel.** All have relevant precedent. The simplest useful next investigation is a small, prospectively frozen **structure-only intervention on an existing completion**: estimate missing landmark locations from supplied personal references registered to observed anchors, and deform only the generated region toward that estimate. Its purpose is to test whether reference geometry contains useful information that the current scaffold misses, while separating that effect from texture and global identity conditioning.

The precise combination below is a candidate implementation difference, not established publication novelty. This search does not establish priority even for that combination. A large experiment or training campaign cannot be justified as a novel-method study yet. A small causal falsification screen can reject the structural premise cheaply; a positive result would justify further mechanism development and a deeper closest-method comparison, not a publication claim.

## Evidence motivating a structural test

The completed [reference/context screen](REFERENCE_CONTEXT_RESULTS_V2.md) improves identity similarity but worsens missing-region MAE and LPIPS against its ResShift scaffold. [Spatially dropped conditioning](SCAFFOLD_CONDITIONING_RESULTS_V1.md) fails much more severely. The [faithfully reproduced dedicated RefFaceInpainting baseline](REFFACE_BASELINE_REVIEW_V1.md) completes sixteen predictions but has blurred or misplaced eyes, nose and lips on the four observed development cases. The native 256 results already contain artifacts. These observations identify a failure to recover target geometry/expression; they do not prove any particular architectural cause.

The already completed external baseline should remain frozen. Its poorer local result does not establish that the published method is generally ineffective, and numerical comparisons against papers' different datasets/protocols are inappropriate.

## Why a component-gate change is insufficient

The pinned author source at commit `0f1ad75677cc8fae4ae14d878e4c6cfce9365f28` was inspected locally: [`Zencoder.py`](https://github.com/WuyangLuo/RefFaceInpainting/blob/0f1ad75677cc8fae4ae14d878e4c6cfce9365f28/networks/Zencoder.py) averages reference feature vectors within semantic classes. [`UnetG.py`](https://github.com/WuyangLuo/RefFaceInpainting/blob/0f1ad75677cc8fae4ae14d878e4c6cfce9365f28/networks/UnetG.py) obtains spatial labels from `Pars2`/`Pars3` on generated features and broadcasts those pooled styles over the predicted labels. `mask_region` suppresses styles for partially surviving classes. The source was read, not edited or executed in this audit.

Removing suppression changes which style features arrive. It does not supply where the person's eye corners, lip boundary or nose should be. Overriding these semantic maps with a reference-derived map would introduce spatial guidance, but it still couples geometry to the texture injector and changes a pathway trained with a different distribution. A positive effect would be difficult to attribute solely to structure. For the next causal screen, a direct coordinate intervention on an unchanged scaffold offers a cleaner separation.

## One candidate: reference landmark consensus transported into a fixed completion

### Hypothesis and scope

When the scaffold already contains the necessary facial components but places or shapes them incorrectly, personal references may improve **static component proportions** through geometry alone, without replacing the scaffold's texture or increasing global identity conditioning. This is a hypothesis about a limited structural precondition. It does not claim recovery of hidden gaze, teeth, mouth state or expression from insufficient evidence.

### Minimal mechanism to freeze before results

1. Extract the same landmark layout from the damaged observation, each supplied reference and the already generated scaffold. Use the observation for target registration anchors. Scaffold landmarks identify the geometry being moved; never treat its hidden landmarks as observed registration targets.
2. Define eligible target anchors only from landmarks whose prescribed local support lies in observed pixels. Freeze the support rule, anchor subset, minimum number and spatial spread. Fit a nonreflecting similarity transformation from each reference's eligible anchors to corresponding observed target anchors. Reject ill-conditioned registration rather than substitute hidden scaffold geometry or clean truth. A similarity transform removes in-plane translation, scale and rotation; it does **not** normalize yaw/pitch or expression.
3. Transform each reference's landmarks into target coordinates. Form one prespecified consensus across the supplied references, such as an equal-weight geometric median. Do not select the best reference, estimator or strength with target scores. Keep all coordinate estimates and registration failures in the receipt.
4. Construct an inverse deformation that maps proposed missing-region landmarks to their existing scaffold locations, with zero displacement on observed pixels and the original hole boundary. Use one fixed regularized smooth solver and interpolation rule. The original mask defines the domain; no new mask-selection/blending policy is introduced.
5. Resample the existing scaffold once inside the hole. Copy outside-mask observation bytes exactly. No reference RGB, semantic texture codes, clean target, gallery, identity evaluator or post-generation score enters this path. Texture is inherited from the scaffold up to the unavoidable effect of resampling.
6. Detect invalid/folding/out-of-domain maps using prespecified numerical checks. Retain rejected attempts and untouched-scaffold fallbacks explicitly; do not count a fallback as a successful structural reconstruction.

This tests a coordinate intervention, distinct from the project's previous reference-routing, image blending, confidence gates, latent feature fusion, context correction and conditioning dropout. The proposed method has not been run in this review. The exact solver, thresholds and settings belong in a frozen protocol, not a retrospective selection from results.

### Laptop feasibility

Existing local landmark ONNX checkpoints and small linear systems could make the screen inexpensive without retraining a diffusion model. This is a feasibility inference, not a measured runtime or memory result. The [official InsightFace landmark adapter](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/blob/master/python-package/insightface/model_zoo/landmark.py) returns regressed landmark coordinates and optionally a pose estimate. It does not return calibrated per-point visibility/confidence in this path. Face-detection confidence must not be relabeled as landmark certainty. Pin the local checkpoint/source hashes before use.

## Identifiability and failure risks

- A visible pixel location does not prove a landmark prediction is reliable: the coordinate regressor uses broad image context and may hallucinate points near holes. Report anchor stability and registration residuals without portraying them as calibrated uncertainty.
- Few or nearly collinear surviving anchors may not constrain the missing geometry. Such cases must stay in coverage/failure accounting.
- Two-dimensional alignment confounds identity shape with out-of-plane pose. Multiple views are not made equivalent by averaging their projected coordinates.
- Mouth opening, smiles, squint and eyebrow state vary within a person. Averaging references may create a state that appears in none of them. Without target evidence, reference consensus is a personal prior, not the hidden target's true expression.
- The conventional 68-point layout has eyelid boundaries, not pupil/iris gaze coordinates or tooth geometry. It cannot support a claim to accurate gaze or teeth reconstruction.
- Deformation cannot create teeth where the scaffold contains closed lips, or correct a topological/semantic error by moving pixels. It may stretch blurred or already wrong content.
- Boundary pinning can overconstrain large displacements and introduce compression, stretching or seams. A zero outside-mask error does not certify good interior geometry.
- A landmark metric derived from the same model used for inference can reward its own bias. Use an independent detector/layout where possible and label both as surrogate measurements; visual/human fidelity remains necessary.
- Repeating four familiar cases is mechanistic development only. It cannot establish broad unknown-person performance, and four identity units cannot support an ordinary two-sided exact significance claim at 0.05.

## Frozen causal comparisons and progression recommendation

Keep observation, masks, references, source generator/checkpoints, seeds, output resolution and evaluator constant. For a four-person initial screen, reuse already observed identities only and keep the eight reserved-final identities unopened.

The minimum causal arms are: unchanged scaffold; no-displacement/resampling integrity control; fixed first-reference geometry; true multi-reference consensus; and a prespecified wrong-person reference set used for geometry only. The wrong-person arm must retain the same reference count and preprocessing and must not be selected by clean-target appearance. A generic/nonpersonal shape control is valuable if it can be frozen without introducing another learned model. This distinguishes personal structure benefit from ordinary smoothing or landmark regularization.

Retain the previously frozen damaged observation, strongest simple preservation/composition control, reference-restoration baseline and dedicated missing-region baseline in the reported comparison. If only ResShift geometry is modified, do not credit that effect to a new generator or claim all backbone baselines received equal compute.

Evaluate FaceNet target and withheld-gallery identity, diagnostic ArcFace, missing-region MAE, LPIPS, SSIM, PSNR, detection/registration coverage, outside-mask byte equality, and a separate landmark/structural error. Report eye/nose/mouth components separately and retain all detector failures with explicit common paired support. Standardized sheets should include every tested identity and seed. Record gaze, eyelids, nose, lips, teeth, mouth state, expression and asymmetry even though this candidate cannot solve all of them.

Before outputs exist, freeze numerical noninferiority tolerances and the required cross-identity/seed benefit. Progress only with repeated personal-structure benefit over the scaffold and appropriate geometry controls, without material identity, pixel, perceptual, preservation or coverage regression. Aggregate improvement driven by one case is insufficient. FaceNet gains with worse MAE/LPIPS or visible expression changes remain a trade-off. A geometry predictor that passes coordinate diagnostics but fails reconstruction fidelity is an unsupported restoration method.

Use paired identity-level estimates and intervals; seeds are repeated measures. Prespecify comparison correction. Treat a four-person screen as a rejection/progression decision, not confirmation of publication-level superiority. Independent development confirmation, stronger nearest-method comparison and real blinded participants are needed if it passes. No final-set use is justified by this report.

## Current novelty statement

**Unsupported as an established novel method; suitable only as a clearly scoped, unvalidated causal hypothesis.** The potentially useful incremental question is whether identity-specific reference coordinates can repair a fixed missing-region synthesis while preserving its appearance and all surviving evidence, and whether this helps beyond single-reference or generic geometry. Neither useful effect nor research priority has been demonstrated. Merely implementing TPS, landmark consensus or reference alignment does not establish novelty.

If the small screen fails, preserve the negative result and distinguish whether failure lies in reference geometry estimation, pose/expression ambiguity, deformation capacity or absent scaffold content. A later method would need a materially different structural representation or a generator that can realize that structure; it must not be relabeled blending or a tuned rerun. If the screen passes, the results would support investing in that next method, not declaring the project publication-ready.
