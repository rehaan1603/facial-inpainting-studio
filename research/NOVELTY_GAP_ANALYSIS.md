## Existing ideas that constrain the claim

[SDEdit](https://sde-image-editing.github.io/) already studies the realism–faithfulness tradeoff through noise level. A denoising-strength sweep is calibration, not a new mechanism. [Differential Diffusion](https://differential-diffusion.github.io/) already provides spatially varying change control and soft inpainting. An editable confidence map or preservation blend alone is not sufficient novelty.

Component dictionaries, warped reference guidance and spatial reference transfer already exist. ReF-LDM and recent semantic/reference-restoration methods substantially overlap the broad goal. The novelty claim is therefore **weaker than a broad 'multi-reference regional fusion' claim**, and that broad claim must not be used.

## What our evidence currently supports

The project contributes a local, reproducible diagnostic comparison with unchanged-input controls, identity-unit statistics and preserved historical baselines. It mechanically implements explicit five-point alignment, regional compatibility weighting and native latent feature injection, but this combination has not demonstrated improvement over global conditioning. Novelty cannot be inferred from an implementation difference that provides no benefit.

A narrower future hypothesis is degradation-dependent preservation with calibrated uncertainty and refusal to replace trustworthy facial evidence, tested against restoration-specific reference baselines. It remains a hypothesis. Confidence maps are manual or heuristic, not validated blind degradation estimation. Robust expression/pose correspondence, external reproduced baselines, identity-disjoint development expansion and blinded human fidelity assessment remain necessary before a novel-method publication claim.
